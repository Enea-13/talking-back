Talking Back is a free app for iPhone, iPad and Android made by [your name]. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Talking Back has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting. Every entry and every Bible verse is built into the app, and the app makes no internet requests of its own.

## What the app keeps on your device

The app remembers a few things in its own storage on your device:

- the responses you save (the Saved list)
- the country you pick for crisis help lines
- your light or dark theme choice, and whether large text is on

No one else can see them, including us. Deleting the app deletes them. If your phone backs up its apps (iCloud Backup, or Google's Android backup), this data may be included in that backup, which Apple or Google keep under your own account.

When you first open a crisis panel, the app suggests a country based on your device's time zone. That guess is made on the device and is not sent anywhere.

## Reading aloud

"Speak it back" uses the text-to-speech voice built into your device. The app does not send the text anywhere.

## Sharing, phone numbers and links

- **Share** opens your device's share sheet with the verse text. You choose whether and where it goes.
- Tapping a help line number opens your phone app so that you can place the call yourself.
- Tapping findahelpline.com opens it in your web browser, where that site's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

Talking Back is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
